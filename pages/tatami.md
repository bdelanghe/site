Tatami Place is a small iPhone app I build for my own travel. It shows the signed-in person's plans on one screen, in four parts: Now, To do, Days and Reference. It is in a private beta on TestFlight, by invitation only.

## How it works

- The plans live in a private trip graph on my own server. The app shows them, and the changes you can make (to-dos and a day's notes) are saved back to them.
- Sign-in uses a passkey. There is no user name or password.
- The app keeps a copy of the plan on the phone, so it still works without a connection.
- Location and notifications are optional, and the app works with both turned off. Arrival notes, Visit log and Reminders are each off until you turn them on in Settings > Tatami.
- There are no purchases, no advertising and nothing shared with other people.
